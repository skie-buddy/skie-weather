Questions, answered honestly

No fine print — Skie is a simple, kind app.

Is it really free?

Yes — completely. No account, no ads, no paywall and no locked features, forever. If you love Skie, you can donate when donations open at launch; it's optional and unlocks nothing, because nothing is locked.

Which phones does Skie work on?

Any Android phone running Android 5.0 or newer — that's almost every Android phone still in use. The download is a 22 MB APK file.

Does Skie track me?

No. Skie has no account and no tracking. Your location is used only to fetch the weather for where you are, at the moment Skie checks it — nothing is sold or shared.

What about iPhone / iOS?

An iOS version is planned, but it's not available yet — iPhone apps have to go through Apple's process. For now, Skie is Android only, and we'd rather tell you that plainly than promise a date we can't keep.

Why does Android warn about “install unknown apps”?

Because this test build is downloaded directly from this page instead of the Play Store, Android shows its standard caution prompt for any such install. It's expected — allow it for your browser just this once, and Skie installs like any other app. The Play Store release later won't need it.
